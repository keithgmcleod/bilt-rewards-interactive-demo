Your Bilt Card questions answered
The Bilt Blue Card has no annual fee† and earns up to 4X at Bilt dining partners, 3X on hotels through Bilt Travel, 3X on Lyft, 2X on flights through Bilt Travel, and 1X on everything else.¹ It’s the entry point for renters and people who love redeeming valuable points for travel. The Bilt Obsidian Card ($95/year†) earns 4X on hotels through Bilt Travel, 3X dining or grocery (up to 6X at Bilt dining partners; grocery up to $25k/year), 3X on flights through Bilt Travel, 2X on other travel, and includes a $100 annual Bilt Travel Hotel credit.¹ Built for cardholders who spend heavily in one food category. The Bilt Palladium Card ($495/year†) earns up to 5X on Bilt dining, 4X on hotels and 3X on flights through Bilt Travel, 4X on Lyft, 2X points on all everyday purchases, provides $200 in annual Bilt Cash, a $400 annual Bilt Travel Hotel credit, and Priority Pass lounge access at 1,300+ airports.¹,² All three cards have no transaction fee on rent, mortgage and HOA and can also earn up to 1.25X points on rent and mortgage payments when housing-only rewards are selected.¹ All multipliers (excluding rent and mortgage) are inclusive of the Bilt loyalty membership and the Bilt Card multiplier earns.
Bilt Cash can be used at Bilt partner merchants, including Lyft, eligible hotel bookings through Bilt Travel, fitness studios like Soul Cycle, Grubhub credits, Walgreens credits, Bilt Collection items, and GoPuff deliveries. Redemption amounts may be subject to per-category limits. These credits are available to all Bilt members, and are accelerated for Bilt cardholders. Bilt Cash can also be used to unlock one-time transfer bonuses, status upgrades, and early access to member experiences and on a point accelerator that adds 1X to your category spend up to $5,000 for the Obsidian and Palladium cards.³ All redemptions are made directly in the Bilt app or on bilt.com. Bilt Cash lasts 12 months from the day you earn it and expires at the end of that month.³
Yes. Every Bilt Card lets you earn rewards on rent and mortgage payments made through Bilt with no transaction fee. If your landlord doesn’t accept credit cards or charges a fee for using a credit card, you’ll be able to avoid the fee using Bilt Card features. The Bilt Card unlocks ACH by providing account and routing numbers, integration with Venmo, and can send a check on your behalf. No transaction fee. No payment portal convenience fee. Your landlord won’t know the difference.
Open the Bilt app and go to the Home tab. Select “Add a mortgage” and search for your servicer. If Bilt supports your servicer directly, you can link your mortgage account and make payments without any additional setup. If your servicer isn’t directly supported, Bilt provides a dedicated BillPay account — including routing and account numbers — that you add as a payment method in your servicer’s portal. The mortgage payment is made using the card, processed by your mortgage provider as a standard ACH transfer to your servicer, transaction fee free, and then the funds are debited from your linked bank account within 24 hours. Allow at least 3 business days for your servicer to receive the payment. Almost all personal mortgage servicers are supported. If your servicer requires a bank name for BillPay, it is “Column N.A. for the benefit of Bilt’s Customers.” Only eligible residential mortgages are supported, while commercial mortgages are not supported at this time.
Your landlord or servicer simply receives a standard payment — nothing indicates any complexity on their end. If you pay via Venmo through Bilt, the payment arrives to your landlord from “Bilt Technologies,” not your personal Venmo account. If you pay via ACH or add the Bilt BillPay account to your payment portal, the payment appears as a standard account transfer from Column N.A. If your servicer requires a bank address, it is: 1100 Gorgas Avenue, Suite A4-700, San Francisco, CA 94129. If Bilt sends a check on your behalf, it is mailed directly to your landlord or property manager at the address you provide.
As a Bilt cardholder, when you pay through Bilt you can bypass your landlord’s credit card fees entirely. Bilt provides an account and routing number that Bilt cardholders can input into their payment portal to avoid transaction fees. The Bilt Card can process the payment to your landlord via ACH providing an account and routing number, Venmo, PayPal, or check — all avoiding a direct card charge. The funds will be debited from your linked bank account. Your landlord receives the same payment they always would.

You can pay rent and mortgage for multiple properties through Bilt simultaneously and earn rewards on all eligible housing payments. There is no annual cap on housing payment amounts and no limit to the number of properties you can pay through Bilt.¹
Bilt Points are rated the most valuable rewards points with The Point Guy valuing them at 2.2 cents each. They are redeemable in several ways, with value varying by redemption method. The highest-value redemptions are typically through Bilt’s 28 airline and hotel transfer partners, which include major domestic and international airline programs and leading hotel loyalty programs. Points transfer at a 1:1 ratio for Bilt travel partners. Other redemption options include: booking travel through the Bilt Travel Portal, applying points toward future rent or mortgage payments, redeeming for Lyft rides, fitness classes, statement credits, eligible student loan payments, and contributing toward a home down payment. For a complete and current list of transfer partners and redemption options, visit bilt.com/rewards.
